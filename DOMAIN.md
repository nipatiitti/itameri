## 2. Facts for content (cite "lähde: HELCOM / SYKE / JNS")

- Brackish: surface salinity ~2 PSU (Perämeri) to ~7 PSU (pääallas), up to 20+ in the Danish
  straits. Mean depth ~55 m, max 459 m (Landsortin syvänne). Nine coastal states, ~85 M people in
  the catchment, water renewal takes ~30 years through the Danish straits.
- Main problem: rehevöityminen (eutrophication) from typpi (N) and fosfori (P) loads. Agriculture is
  the largest Finnish source, then wastewater, forestry, atmospheric deposition. Consequences:
  sinilevä (cyanobacteria) blooms in July-August, hapettomat pohjat (hypoxic bottoms; ~1/4 of the
  deep bottoms of the pääallas), internal loading releasing P from sediments, loss of rakkohauru
  (bladderwrack, Fucus vesiculosus) and meriajokas (eelgrass, Zostera marina) meadows.
- JNS story points: founded 1992 for maritime heritage, Clean Baltic Sea projects since 2004;
  biggest single win was phosphorus removal at Pietari (St Petersburg) wastewater plants; kipsi
  (gypsum) field treatment cuts P runoff roughly in half; lähikala / cyprinid fishing removes
  nutrients; BalticReed harvests ruoko (reed); Baltic Seagrass restores meriajokas; SeaToo; clean
  shipping (CargoRes, chemical tankers); Itämeripäivä (Baltic Sea Day) is the last Thursday of
  August (27 Aug 2026); maps, books, art, old sea charts.
- Species for quizzes/games (fi / en): silakka herring · kilohaili sprat · turska cod · kuha
  zander · ahven perch · hauki pike · lohi salmon · kampela flounder · itämerennorppa Baltic
  ringed seal · halli grey seal · pyöriäinen harbour porpoise (critically endangered, ~500) ·
  merikotka white-tailed eagle · haahka eider · merimetso cormorant · sinisimpukka blue mussel ·
  rakkohauru bladderwrack · meriajokas eelgrass · punalevät red algae. Invasives: mustatäplätokko
  round goby · petovesikirppu fishhook waterflea · villasaksirapu Chinese mitten crab ·
  amerikansukasjalkainen Marenzelleria. Cyanobacteria: Nodularia spumigena (toxic), Aphanizomenon.
- Everyday actions (pledge lists): eat Baltic fish (silakka, särki), fewer meat days, no
  fertiliser near shores, septic tanks on cottages, don't flush medicines, boat holding tanks
  (septi), pick litter, donate, Itämeripäivä.
- Policy timeline: HELCOM 1974/1992 convention · Baltic Sea Action Plan 2007, updated 2021 ·
  EU Marine Strategy 2008 · Finnish Itämerihaaste 2007 · JNS 1992 · Clean Baltic Sea 2004.
